## Project Overview

Business performance goes beyond knowing how much a company sells. Understanding what drives revenue, which customer segments contribute most, where profits are generated, and where operational risks exist is essential for informed decision-making.

This project explores these questions through an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard built to analyse sales performance, customer segments, product profitability, regional performance, and delivery operations.

The analysis combines data transformation, data modelling, DAX calculations, time-based performance analysis, and interactive visualisation to translate business data into meaningful insights.

**Project Objective:** Develop an interactive business intelligence solution that enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify business patterns, and investigate opportunities for improvement.

## Business Questions Investigated

The analysis was guided by the following business questions:

1. How is revenue distributed across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customer segments?
2. Which products and product categories contribute most to business performance and profitability?
3. Which regions generate the highest sales?
4. How has sales, profit, and quantity performance changed compared with the previous year?
5. Which regions have the highest concentration of late delivery risks?
6. How does monthly sales performance fluctuate throughout the year?
7. What patterns in customer contribution, product profitability, and regional performance deserve further business investigation?

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI solution consists of three navigable report pages, combining executive reporting with more detailed performance analysis.

### 1. Executive Overview

A consolidated view of business performance designed to help stakeholders quickly assess key performance indicators and identify areas requiring further investigation.

Key components include:

- Total Sales, Total Quantity, Total Profit, and Average Delivery Time KPIs.
- Year-over-year performance comparisons.
- Revenue contribution by customer segment.
- Top 10 performing products by sales.
- Sales distribution across regions and markets.

**Key purpose:** Provide management with a high-level understanding of business performance and highlight areas for deeper analysis.

### 2. Customer & Regional Analysis

A detailed examination of customer and regional sales contributions.

This page is designed to investigate:

- Regional sales distribution across customer segments.
- Differences in Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office contributions.
- Comparative sales performance across geographical markets.
- Monthly sales trend

**Key purpose:** Help stakeholders understand how revenue is distributed across customer segments and geographical markets.

### 3. Performance Analytics

A deeper analytical view focused on product profitability, operational performance, and sales trends.

Key components include:

- Profit breakdown by product category.
- Count of late delivery risks by region.
- Monthly sales performance trends.
- Interactive filtering by region, product category, customer segment, quarter, and month.

**Key purpose:** Provide a more detailed view of profitability patterns, regional delivery risks, and monthly sales performance.

### Customer Segment Contribution

Consumer customers accounted for 52% of total sales, followed by Corporate customers at 30% and Home Office customers at 18%.

**Business implication:** Consumer customers represent the largest revenue segment. Management could investigate the factors behind this contribution and identify opportunities to strengthen revenue from the other customer segments.

### Product Category Profitability

Fan Shop recorded the highest category profit at approximately $1.83M, followed by Apparel at approximately $881.88K and Golf at approximately $497.52K.

**Business implication:** The difference in category profitability provides an opportunity to investigate the factors contributing to stronger and weaker category performance.

### Regional Sales Performance

Western Europe recorded approximately $5.89M in sales, making it the leading region in the displayed regional sales comparison.

**Business implication:** Its contribution makes Western Europe an important region for further investigation into the factors associated with its sales performance.

### Delivery Risk Distribution

The regional comparison revealed differences in the count of late delivery risks, with Central America and Western Europe recording the highest counts in the displayed analysis.

**Business implication:** These regions provide useful starting points for investigating delivery-related operational challenges and understanding where further attention may be required.

### Year-over-Year Business Growth

Sales increased by 37.9%, profit by 38.8%, and quantity sold by 28.7% compared with the previous year.

**Business implication:** The positive movement across these indicators provides a basis for investigating the drivers of business growth and assessing whether profitability is keeping pace with sales expansion.

## Business Recommendations & Further Investigation

Based on the findings, the following areas warrant further investigation:

1. **Customer Segment Growth:** Investigate opportunities to increase Corporate and Home Office revenue while sustaining Consumer segment performance.
2. **Product Category Performance:** Examine the factors contributing to differences in category profitability and identify potential improvement opportunities.
3. **Regional Performance:** Investigate the commercial drivers behind Western Europe's leading sales contribution.
4. **Delivery Operations:** Prioritise further investigation of regions with higher late delivery risk counts to understand the underlying operational challenges.
5. **Business Growth Monitoring:** Track sales, profit, and quantity growth together to assess the sustainability and quality of business performance.

These recommendations identify opportunities for further analysis rather than assuming causes that have not been established by the available data.
